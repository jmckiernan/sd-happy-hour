## Neighborhoods Built for Patio Life

San Diego's climate basically demands outdoor seating year-round, and a few neighborhoods lean into that harder than most:

- **North Park & South Park** — Craft beer country, with sprawling patios and a laid-back, artsy crowd that treats dogs like regulars.
- **Ocean Beach** — Beachy, unpretentious, and full of casual spots where a leash and a sandy paw are basically dress code.
- **Liberty Station** — Wide open walkways and grassy patches between eateries make it an easy stroll-and-sip kind of afternoon.
- **South Park/Golden Hill corridor** — Smaller, neighborhood-y bars with sidewalk seating that dogs seem to gravitate toward.

Each of these areas has multiple bars and restaurants with patio or sidewalk seating where dogs are typically welcome — but "typically" is doing some work in that sentence, so always double check before you settle in (more on that below).

## What Makes a Happy Hour Actually Dog-Friendly

Not every patio with outdoor tables is genuinely set up for pups. Here's what to look for:

**Shade and water.** A good dog-friendly spot usually has a water bowl out without you having to ask, and at least some shaded seating so your dog isn't panting on hot concrete.

**Space to settle in.** The best patios give dogs room to lie down without being underfoot of servers or other guests — string lights and turf patches are a good sign you're in the right place.

**A staff that means it.** Some places tolerate dogs; others genuinely love them. You'll know the difference by whether the bartender asks your dog's name before yours.
