## The short answer

If you want one plan for Tuesday, **[August 25](/blog/date/2026-08-25/)**: head to the Gaslamp Quarter, where Fifth Avenue turns into a 13-court pickleball venue with DJ Spitz, a non-alcoholic Zero-Proof Garden, and wristband-access food and drink specials at nearby restaurants. If you'd rather stay local and low-key, North Park's Mabel's Gone Fishing is pouring "dirty drinks" for a good cause, and Carmel Valley has a free concert if you want something quieter. Here's how the day (and the week around it) breaks down.

## Tonight's headliner: Urban Pickleball takes over the Gaslamp

The six-week **Urban Pickleball** series has expanded this year to 13 courts spanning three blocks of Fifth Avenue between Island Avenue and K Street. Expect ladder-style open play for all skill levels, live music from DJ Spitz, a Sports Market with apparel and gear, and a Spin-to-Win prize wheel. The real draw for a happy-hour crowd: registration includes a wristband good for "Paddle Hour" food and drink specials at participating Gaslamp restaurants and bars, plus a dedicated non-alcoholic space (the Zero-Proof Garden) with kombucha and hydration stations if you're pacing yourself.

Tuesday's session runs from 4:00 PM to roughly 7:20 PM, and tickets run **$40–$45**. Given the multi-week format, double-check dates and pricing before you go — [full details and other session dates via San Diego Reader](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/25/gaslamp-quarter-urban-pickleball/?et=364343).

## Sip with a purpose: Dirty Drinks week at Mabel's Gone Fishing

Through August 30, Mabel's Gone Fishing on 30th Street is part of **Dirty Drinks**, a national campaign from Zero Foodprint that ties bar menus back to regenerative farming. Order a specially crafted "dirty drink" and $1 gets kicked to farmers building healthier, carbon-storing soil. It's a low-effort way to make your bar tab do a little more — no special reservation needed, just order the featured drink when you're there. Cost of the drinks themselves wasn't listed, so ask your bartender what's on the Dirty Drinks menu that night. [Details and campaign background](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/dirty-drinks-15b38940/?et=365081).

## Family plan: Build a Lego city in San Marcos

If you've got kids (or just really like Lego), North City in San Marcos is running a community build from **Saturday, August 22 through Saturday, August 29**, inviting families to add to a growing Lego city piece by piece. It's free and open to all ages, making it an easy daytime add-on before an evening out elsewhere in the county. [Event info](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/north-city-invites-san-diego-famili/?et=365054).

## Free and quiet: chamber music in Carmel Valley

For something calmer than Gaslamp pickleball, the Friends of the Carmel Valley Library are hosting a free hour-long concert, **Hybrid Waves from the Pacific Rim & Beyond**, featuring flutist Elena Yarritu, pianist Cho-Hyun Park, and violinist Christian Gonzalez performing works by NWEAMO composers. It starts at 6:30 PM and runs about an hour — a solid pick if you want live music without a cover charge or a crowd. [More on the program](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/25/hybrid-waves-from-the-pacific-rim-a/?et=365032).

## For writers: three online workshops worth knowing about

San Diego's literary calendar has a cluster of online classes this week, all hosted virtually so location isn't a factor:

- **Sneaking in the Backdoor of Poetry and Prose** — a low-pressure three-week class starting at 2:00 PM Tuesday, using prompts and mind-mapping to loosen up your writing. Runs $81–$94.50. [Class details](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/25/sneaking-in-the-backdoor-of-poetry-/?et=362158).
- **Book Marketing for Authors** — a six-week course starting at 5:30 PM Tuesday for independent authors building a marketing plan around a book launch. Runs $162–$189. [Course info](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/25/book-marketing-for-authors/?et=360837).
- **Write a Winning Nonfiction Book Proposal** — starts the next evening, Wednesday, **[August 26](/blog/date/2026-08-26/)**, at 5:30 PM, for writers with a completed or in-progress manuscript. This one's 18+ and pricier at $270–$315, with weekly instructor critique. [Workshop details](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/write-a-winning-nonfiction-book-pro/?et=361716).
