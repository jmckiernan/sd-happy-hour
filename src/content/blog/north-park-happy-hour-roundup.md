North Park doesn't do one kind of happy hour — it does all of them within a few blocks of University and 30th, which is exactly why it's worth the drive from the coast.

Start serious at **Polite Provisions**, one of the better cocktail bars in the city on any night, with $9 craft cocktails and $5 draft beers Tuesday through Friday from 4-6pm on the patio. It's the kind of list where the bartenders actually know what's in your glass.

If you'd rather keep it loose, **Coin-Op Game Room** has $5 beers and $6 wells from 4-7pm Monday through Friday, plus free arcade games included — Skee-Ball and a happy hour deal is a genuinely underrated combination for a Tuesday.

For something in between, **Neighborhood** runs $4 beers, $5 wells, and $2 off appetizers from 3-6pm on weekdays, in a lower-key gastropub setting that's easy to just show up to without a plan.

All three are walkable from each other, so a North Park happy hour crawl is very doable — just budget extra time if you're starting at Polite Provisions, since that patio fills up first.
