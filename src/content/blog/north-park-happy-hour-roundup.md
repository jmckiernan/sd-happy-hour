[North Park](/neighborhoods/north-park/) does not have one kind of happy hour. Around University Avenue and 30th Street, you can choose a serious cocktail, a casual beer, appetizers, or arcade games without leaving the neighborhood’s central blocks.

The three spots below are close enough to combine, but their schedules are not identical. Check the venue details and confirm the current offer before you go.

## Polite Provisions: craft cocktails on the patio

**Polite Provisions** is the cocktail-focused choice. Its current happy hour listing runs Tuesday through Friday from 4–6 PM with $9 craft cocktails and $5 draft beers.

Go here when the drink itself is the point rather than an add-on to dinner. The patio is popular, so an earlier arrival gives you a better chance at the seat you want. If your group prefers a quieter conversation, Tuesday or Wednesday will generally be a better bet than Friday.

**Best for:** cocktail fans, date night, and a polished first stop.

## Coin-Op Game Room: beer, wells, and arcade games

**Coin-Op Game Room** takes the opposite approach: the activity is built in. The current weekday happy hour runs from 4–7 PM with $5 beers, $6 wells, and free arcade games.

The later end time makes it the easiest second stop on a small North Park crawl. It also works for a group that wants something to do besides sit at a table. Expect more noise and movement than at a cocktail bar.

**Best for:** groups, casual dates, and anyone who wants games with the deal.

## Neighborhood: a straightforward gastropub stop

**Neighborhood** currently lists happy hour from 3–6 PM, Monday through Friday, with $4 beers, $5 wells, and $2 off appetizers.

It is the flexible option when your group wants familiar drinks and food in a lower-key setting. The 3 PM start also makes it useful before the central North Park bars hit their busiest period.

**Best for:** an early start, appetizers, and mixed groups.

## How to plan a North Park happy hour crawl

Start at Neighborhood at 3 PM, move to Polite Provisions when its deals begin at 4 PM, and save Coin-Op for last because its listed window runs until 7 PM. You do not need to complete all three; the route simply works with the current time windows.

Parking near 30th and University becomes more competitive after work. Park once and walk, or use a rideshare if everyone wants to participate in the cocktail list. Check each destination’s entry requirements and current menu before setting out.

## Quick comparison

- Choose **Polite Provisions** for the strongest cocktail focus.
- Choose **Coin-Op Game Room** for a social activity and the latest end time.
- Choose **Neighborhood** for an early, uncomplicated gastropub happy hour.
