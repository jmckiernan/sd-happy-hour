If you only know one San Diego neighborhood for happy hour, make it Little Italy. Between India St and Kettner Blvd, there's a serious deal within a five-minute walk of almost anywhere you stand — the hard part is picking where to sit down.

**Ironside Fish & Oyster** is the classic starting point: $1.50 oysters and $6 beers, Monday through Friday from 3-6pm, at the raw bar or the patio out front. A block away, **Craft & Commerce** runs $8 cocktails and $5 draft beers on the same weekday window, plus half-price snacks if you want something to go with your drink.

For something with more spice, **Bracero Cocina** pours $8 margaritas alongside $5 tacos and $6 Mexican beers, Tuesday through Friday from 4-6pm — order the al pastor if it's on the board that day. And if you want to close the night out somewhere nicer without paying nicer prices, **Herb & Wood** does $8 cocktails, $6 wines, and $5 beers from 4-6pm on weekdays, in a dining room that usually costs a lot more to sit in after 6.

None of these require a reservation for happy hour specifically, but Little Italy fills up fast on Thursday and Friday — get there before 5pm if you want a seat instead of standing room at the bar.
