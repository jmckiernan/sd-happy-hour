[Little Italy](/neighborhoods/little-italy/) is one of San Diego’s easiest neighborhoods for building a happy hour around more than one stop. India Street and Kettner Boulevard put raw bars, cocktail rooms, Mexican restaurants, and polished dining rooms within a compact, walkable grid.

The listings below reflect the current schedule in our directory. Deals change, and some restaurants limit happy hour to a bar or patio, so open the venue page and confirm before visiting.

## Ironside Fish & Oyster: oysters and an early start

**Ironside Fish & Oyster** is a natural first stop for seafood. Its current weekday happy hour runs from 3–6 PM with $1.50 oysters, $6 beers, $8 wines, and $9 cocktails.

The early start makes Ironside useful when you want to beat the after-work rush. The raw bar and front patio each have a different feel, so ask where the happy hour menu is available before settling in.

**Best for:** oysters, an early afternoon start, and seafood fans.

## Craft & Commerce: cocktails and shareable snacks

**Craft & Commerce** currently runs happy hour from 4–6 PM, Monday through Friday. The listing includes $8 cocktails, $5 draft beers, $6 wines, and half-price snacks.

It works well for a group with mixed drink preferences, and it is close enough to Ironside to make the two an easy pairing. The room can get loud later in the week, which is a plus for an energetic night and less ideal for a quiet conversation.

**Best for:** cocktails, groups, and a livelier room.

## Bracero Cocina: margaritas and tacos

**Bracero Cocina** brings a Mexican option to the Kettner corridor. Its listed happy hour is Tuesday through Friday from 4–6 PM, with $8 margaritas, $5 tacos, and $6 Mexican beers.

Choose it when food is as important as the first round. Since day-specific menu items can rotate, check the current board or ask the server what is included when you arrive.

**Best for:** tacos, margaritas, and a food-forward stop.

## Herb & Wood: a polished finish

**Herb & Wood** currently lists weekday happy hour from 4–6 PM with $8 cocktails, $6 wines, and $5 beers. The dining room feels more formal than a typical bar, making it a good finish when you want happy-hour pricing without giving up date-night atmosphere.

It also gets busy as dinner begins. Arrive closer to 4 PM or ask whether a reservation affects access to the happy hour menu.

**Best for:** date night, wine, and an upscale setting.

## A simple Little Italy happy hour plan

For two stops, begin at 3 PM at Ironside, then walk to Craft & Commerce, Bracero, or Herb & Wood when their 4 PM windows begin. That sequence gives you time to order without racing the clock.

The County Center/Little Italy trolley stop and Waterfront Park are convenient anchors. Thursday and Friday parking tightens before dinner, so transit or a rideshare is often easier than moving a car between stops.

## Before you go

- Confirm that the offer is running on your specific day.
- Ask whether happy hour is bar-only, patio-only, or available throughout the restaurant.
- Check the end time before ordering a final round.
- Use the venue’s official website link for the latest menu or reservation policy.
