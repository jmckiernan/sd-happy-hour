A good date night doesn't have to mean an 8pm reservation and a three-figure bill. Timed right, happy hour gets you the same atmosphere — sometimes better — for a lot less.

**Raised by Wolves** in UTC is the move if you want to feel like you found a secret: a hidden speakeasy behind an unmarked door, with $10 classic cocktails and $8 beer and wine, Tuesday through Thursday from 5-7pm. It's a whole experience before the first sip.

If tiki is more your speed, **False Idol** in Little Italy pours $10 tiki cocktails with half-price pupus, Wednesday and Thursday from 5-7pm, in a dark, plant-covered room that doesn't feel like San Diego at all — in a good way.

For something a little different, **Kindred** in South Park is a vegan cocktail bar with genuine personality: $6 beers, $8 cocktails, and $5 vegan bites, Monday through Friday from 4-6pm. Even if you're not vegan, the food holds up.

And if you want the date to feel a little more grown-up, **Herb & Wood** does $8 cocktails, $6 wines, and $5 beers from 4-6pm on weekdays in one of Little Italy's nicer dining rooms — worth booking a table for even during happy hour, since it fills up.

Any of these work solo too, but they're built for two.
