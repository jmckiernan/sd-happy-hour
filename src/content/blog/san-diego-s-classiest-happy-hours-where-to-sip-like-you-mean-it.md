## What Makes a Happy Hour "Classy," Anyway?

It's less about the price tag and more about the details:

- **A real wine and cocktail program** — not just well drinks, but a thoughtfully curated list with a few things you've never heard of.
- **Small plates that feel like actual food** — think oysters, charcuterie, or a well-dressed crudo, not just a bowl of pretzels.
- **A room with good bones** — soft lighting, a view, an actual bar worth sitting at.
- **Service that doesn't rush you** — the best classy happy hours feel like an invitation to linger, not a countdown clock.

## Neighborhoods Worth Exploring

**Little Italy** is a solid bet if you're chasing an upscale-but-unpretentious vibe — lots of Italian-leaning restaurants with patio seating and wine-forward menus that lend themselves naturally to a refined evening out.

**La Jolla** leans coastal-elegant. Ocean views, seafood-heavy menus, and a slightly dressed-up crowd make it a natural fit for anyone who wants their happy hour to feel like a mini occasion.

**Downtown/Gaslamp** has its share of rowdy sports bars, sure, but it also hides some genuinely polished hotel bars and steakhouse lounges where the happy hour crowd skews a little more buttoned-up.

**North Park and South Park** are worth a mention too — not "classy" in the white-tablecloth sense, but full of wine bars and cocktail dens with a curated, low-key sophistication that feels elevated without trying too hard.

## How to Happy-Hour Classy on a Budget

The whole point of happy hour, even the fancy kind, is that you're getting a taste of the good stuff without the full-price commitment. A few tips:

- Wine bars often run happy hour specials on glasses or flights — a great way to sample without committing to a bottle.
- Hotel lounges and steakhouse bars sometimes have happy hour menus that undersell how nice the space actually is.
- Look for spots with a raw bar or oyster program — happy hour oyster specials are one of the best ways to eat well without spending big.
