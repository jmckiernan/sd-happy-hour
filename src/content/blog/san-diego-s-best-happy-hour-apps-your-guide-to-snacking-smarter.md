## What Makes an App Menu Happy-Hour Worthy

Not all discounted snacks are created equal. Here's what separates a solid happy hour spread from a sad one:

- **Something crispy** — fries, tots, calamari, or fried anything. A good HH menu always has a crunchy anchor.
- **A shareable centerpiece** — think flatbreads, nachos, or a big plate of something meant for the table.
- **One unexpected item** — a spot that throws in something a little elevated (truffle oil, a house-made sauce, local seafood) shows they're not phoning it in.
- **Portions that make sense for the price** — the best deals feel generous, not like a sample platter.

## Neighborhoods Worth Exploring

San Diego's happy hour scene is spread out, and each pocket of the city has its own personality when it comes to bar food.

**Gaslamp Quarter** leans into the lively, high-energy crowd — expect bar staples done well, with plenty of patio seating for people-watching while you snack.

**North Park** tends to attract a more laid-back, craft-beer-forward crowd, and the food often follows suit with elevated pub fare and creative small plates.

**Little Italy** is your move if you're craving something a little more polished — think shareable Italian-leaning bites alongside the usual happy hour suspects.

**Pacific Beach** and the coastal spots are all about casual, beachy vibes — fish tacos, ceviche, and anything you can eat with your hands while watching the sunset.
