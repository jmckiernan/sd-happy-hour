Everything else — a card show, live piano, two online writing classes, a craft session, a surf documentary, and a Schubert salon — clusters between 5 and 10:30 p.m., which means you can realistically pair one with dinner and still make it home at a reasonable hour.

- **Morning:** Free Alzheimer's caregiving workshop (in Spanish), Otay Mesa

- **Anytime from your couch:** Two online writing workshops start Wednesday evening

As always with independently listed events, confirm times, cost, and age restrictions directly with the venue before heading out — details can shift after publication.

## Morning: A Free Caregiver Workshop in Otay Mesa

If you're supporting someone with Alzheimer's or dementia, the **Otay Mesa-Nestor Library** hosts "[Alzheimer's San Diego: Estrategias para la Planificación para la Alimentación](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/alzheimers-san-diego-estrategias-pa-fc816130/?et=363391)" from 10 to 11:30 a.m. It's a Spanish-language session on feeding strategies for caregivers, free and open to all ages, at 3003 Coronado Avenue. Good option if you need practical caregiving guidance without a fee attached.

## Late Afternoon: Cards, Then Everything Else Kicks In

Starting at 5 p.m. and running until 10 p.m., **Dave & Buster's** in Mission Valley turns into a trading-card marketplace for the "[Card Show](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/dave-busters-card-show/?et=364471)." Expect vendor tables with singles, graded cards, sealed product, and vintage pieces. It's free and all-ages, so it works as a low-commitment stop before or after dinner at 2931 Camino del Rio North — and you're already at an arcade if the kids need a break from browsing bins.

Also at 5 p.m., pianist **Kevin Ryan** performs at **L'Auberge Del Mar** (1540 Camino Del Mar). The Reader listing doesn't specify cost, end time, or age policy for "[Kevin Ryan](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/kevin-ryan-033761dc/?et=363003)," so call the hotel or check with the venue directly if you're planning a night out around it — this reads more like ambient hotel-lounge music than a ticketed show, but that's worth confirming.

## From Your Couch: Two Online Writing Workshops

Writers have two overlapping-but-different options Wednesday evening, both hosted online.

"[Write a Winning Nonfiction Book Proposal](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/write-a-winning-nonfiction-book-pro/?et=361716)" runs 5:30 to 8 p.m. and costs $270–$315 for what's described as a multi-week workshop where students draft proposal sections weekly and get instructor critique. It's aimed at adults (18+) with a completed or in-progress nonfiction manuscript — bring a laptop with Word, Google Docs, or Scrivener.

Running the same evening, 6 to 8 p.m., "[Writing the Sacred Ordinary](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/writing-the-sacred-ordinary-20755174/?et=362155)" is a gentler, single-session class ($36–$42, all ages) built around noticing everyday beauty rather than processing trauma or news cycles. If you want a lower-stakes writing exercise without the multi-week commitment, this is the one.

## North Park: A Craft Session With a Drink in Hand

**Mike Hess Brewing North Park** hosts "[Summer Crushed Glass SunCatchers](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/patchworks-presents-summer-crushed-/?et=364655)" from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m., run by Patchwork. For $28, you pick from four summer-themed designs and build a crushed-glass resin suncatcher — no experience required. It's all-ages and pairs naturally with a beer at the brewery, at 3812 Grim Avenue.

## Oceanside: A Surf Documentary Far From the Usual Break

The **Sunshine Brooks Theatre** in Oceanside screens "[Big Wednesday: The Rising Dawn Film Screening](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/big-wednesday-the-rising-dawn-film-/?et=364404)" from 6:30 to 8 p.m. The documentary follows a new surfing movement forming in Côte d'Ivoire — a fitting pick given the "Big Wednesday" framing. Tickets run $15, all ages, at 217 N. Coast Highway.

## La Jolla: An Intimate Evening With Schubert

For a quieter, more formal close to the night, **Baker-Baum Concert Hall** presents "[Schubertiade](https://www.sandiegoreader.com/events/2026/aug/26/schubertiade/?et=359114)" starting at 7 p.m. The program recreates the salon settings where Schubert's music was first performed. Cost isn't listed publicly, so check directly with the venue at 7600 Fay Avenue before you go — it's marked all-ages, but salon-style classical programs tend to skew adult in practice.
